The surprising origin of the word dashboard

Recently, I found out the true origin of the word dashboard, and it blew my mind.

In the data age, almost everyone has access to dashboards, which give an overview of important information, such as KPIs.

I'm pretty sure that everyone knows that the word dashboard is also used in transportation, like in cars and planes, where drivers and pilots have an overview of the speed, fuel reserves and outside temperature.

But the word dashboard is much older than cars...

Early cars were seen as horseless carriages and words were often simply taken over from horse-powered transportation — horsepower being the perfect example.

"Dash" has many uses and meanings, and one of them is "to splash" — I'd like my coffee with a dash of milk — and to stop a coach driver from getting dashed with mud, a wooden board was attached in front of them, hence the "dash board".

Car designers later added gauges and controls to the dashboard, and we now look at dashboards on our computers, with no mud in sight.
